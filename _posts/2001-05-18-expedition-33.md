# Review
I know that I'm a minority in saying that this game is bad. As more people praise that its GoTY material, I cope and come up with reasons to not like the game even more. I spent almost 100hr just to make sure I am informed enough to hate the game. Petty old man.

## Parry
You might think that I dislike the game because its a turn based game but I grew up with pokemon, digimon2, and I'm old so Turn Based is my jam. The thing I hate the most in the game is the parry mechanic. 

A game will have a CWL- Challenge Win Lose (there's a proper term for this but I forgot) so lets discuss what is the challenges in games.
- In Pokemon games, the main challenge is to learn the enemy pokemon's element so you hit their weakness. Yes it's dumb that new pokemon game just gives you the weakness and I'm not saying pokemon is a good game. But this core mechanic is in line with the pokemon's core which is er.. the pokemon.
- In HSR, a gacha game where you collect so so many characters, the main challenge is building the right team, choosing the right character for the fight.
- _I can go on_
Expedition 33 has a lot of mechanics: element, lumina, equipment, stats, characters that each have their own unique things, etc. But in every fight I never feel like I need to care about any of those, the only thing that I need to do is learn how to [**Parry**](https://mangadex.org/title/42f62ab4-8fdb-4710-956d-999e3c55f7ad/i-parry-everything-what-do-you-mean-i-m-the-strongest-i-m-not-even-an-adventurer-yet). 

I know there are insanely OP to wacky builds you can make but the game doesn't really nudge you to that direction. Going in blind, you don't know what Pictos you're gonna get so there's no planning ahead. When lets say you're struggling to a Charm mechanic, so you decide that you need an Anti-Charm Pictos, where do you find that? The only obvious thing you CAN do is just dodge better, parry better.

In pokemon, you can win with just 1 pokemon aka your starter and that's fine. You ignored all other mechanics because you like this one pokemon so much. In exp33, you can ignore every mechanic because you love... parrying? that's not a good signal.

## Story
While I think parry mechanic is an objective criticism, this next part is very much not. I'm old. That means I have experienced a lot of things and things are rarely fresh anymore. I truly cherish whenever I encounter an original thing and 33 is what I call "fake new". There are variations of fake-new but this time its about bringing an old concept and changing/hiding enough things to make it feel new. The very simplest form of this story is about the good of the many vs the good of the few(the MC). Then, obscuring layers are added like how this is a magical world, inside a painting, from an old time, and French. 

There is nothing wrong in being fake-new, as long as you bring something new along with it. Avatar (blue people) is just reskinned Pocahontas with extra steps and I love it. It is not about the story, it separates itself by making it about the world, the creatures, the floating island, the CGI, and I can go days talking James Cameron. The vibe that I get, is that 33 thinks their story is original. It uses classical music and dark colors which usually means serious business. The end of the first act combines the combat with cutscene, signalling that I need to pay attention to the story even more. But the story are simply not deep enough. Why is the painter doing a number counting down? Why from 100? What happens when it reaches 0? The world is practically gone before it reaches 0 as all the reproductive age has been Gommage-d. There might be an answer for this, but anything can be the answer. This is a magical world, inside a world with magic so we can make the rules as we go. In the end, the story feels like it was taken from [**Reddit**](https://www.reddit.com/r/im14andthisisdeep/).

My other pain point is that the story didn't need to be set in a handwavy-magic world. It could've worked in a futuristic setting where instead of painting, its a server. The characters can be AI assistants that copies the owners behavior and the other characters are AI agents that has achieved sentience. It make sense that servers cannot run indefinitely cause why can't a magical family just magic everything. Whats that? Tech is boring? Thats been done before? Exactly why its a fake-new.

## Relationship
I love Genshin Impact and Borderlands 2 because I love elements. Specifically I love the interactions between elements (yes I also like Slime Rancher). Exp33 has great story-emotional bonding between the characters but its not reflected in the game elements. Characters mainly interacts with their own skills and you rarely need to setup for the other party member, maybe healing. There are moments where you attack together as a party but when was that? That's right, when you PARRY.

Also, to build relationship and unlock new abilities you simply talk to them in the camp. An XCOM style system would've been better where you gain relationship points for every battle. Gives you more reason to grind is never a bad thing, grinding is only grinding when you hate doing it.

## Finale
So I don't like the combat and I don't like the story. I don't enjoy the world since I don't feel its grounded enough. What else is there to like? How did I stay for 100hrs? Boobs.
